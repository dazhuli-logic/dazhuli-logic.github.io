I am an Associate Professor of Logic at the Institute of Philosophy, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the Department of Philosophy, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research focuses on modal logic, dynamic logic, and epistemic logic, together with applications of logic to philosophy, games, social networks, theoretical computer science, and AI. At the current stage, I am particularly interested in (1) logics for graph games; (2) logics for dependence and causality; (3) modal approaches towards mereology; (4) new foundations for epistemic logic and uncertainty reasoning; (5) monotonicity reasoning.

I am currently accepting applications from prospective master’s students and am also open to research collaborations. Prospective students and potential collaborators are welcome to contact me by email.
