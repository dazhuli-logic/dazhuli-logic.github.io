Dazhu Li

I am an Associate Professor of Logic at the Institute of Philosophy, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the Department of Philosophy, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research focuses on modal logic, dynamic logic, and epistemic logic, together with applications of logic to philosophy, games, and social networks. I completed my PhD in Logic at Tsinghua University and the Institute for Logic, Language and Computation at the University of Amsterdam.

I was a visiting scholar at Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń in 2023 and 2025.

Please see the research page for my research interests, current manuscripts, and publications.
